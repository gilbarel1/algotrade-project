Meet the team

Four specialists, each with a single job and its own guardrails. No agent sees more than it needs; heavy data never touches the language model.

📈

Technical Agent

Reads adjusted OHLC and a suite of indicators computed server-side, then narrates the setup — trend, momentum and volatility — in plain language.

📰

Sentiment Agent

Scores the news in two ways at once — an LLM read and a finance model (FinBERT for English, HeBERT for Hebrew). Where they disagree is a signal, not noise.

🧾

Earnings Agent

Pulls disclosures from Maya (TASE) and SEC EDGAR (US). Reports a figure only when it appears verbatim — self-consistency voting marks anything unclear ambiguous rather than inventing it.

🛡️

Risk Manager

Three passes, always: a draft call, a devil's-advocate critique that attacks it, then a final decision that answers the critique. Conviction is capped when the signals conflict.

How a call gets made

One watchlist in, one recommendation report out — with every step of the reasoning kept on the page.

  1. 1

    Fan out

    The orchestrator runs the watchlist in parallel — the technical, sentiment and earnings agents work each ticker at once.

  2. 2

    Gather the evidence

    Prices, dual-scored news and verbatim earnings figures are collected per name — short text and scores only; the raw data stays in the store.

  3. 3

    Critique the thesis

    The Risk Manager drafts a call, argues against it, then commits — so the weak points are on the record, not buried.

  4. 4

    Write the report

    A PDF lands with a per-ticker page, the dual-sentiment panel, the full reasoning trace and citations back to every source.
